Infiniti Account Portal

Use the portal to create an account, sign in securely, manage 2FA, and move to KYC, transactions, favourites, and admin review with a shared session.

  • Usability: clear sections, direct guidance, and fewer dead ends.
  • Clarity: visible account state and action-specific feedback.
  • Authentication: safer sign-in flow with 2FA cues and session controls.
  • Performance: fewer duplicate requests and lighter DOM updates.
  • Security: reduced token exposure and safer QR rendering.
